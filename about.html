Approach

Every reading on this site comes from a written rule, and every rule names its source. There is no AI text generation and no hidden scoring: open any Why? panel to see the stars that triggered a statement and the classical line behind it. (Hovering over “Why?” shows the internal rule ID, which is handy when reporting a problem.)

  • One school, stated openly. The analysis follows the 三合 (three-harmony) method of 《紫微斗數全書》, the classical text most schools descend from. Where schools disagree, the app says so and, where it matters, offers a setting.
  • The classics first. Pattern definitions follow the 全書's own wording. Where modern sources use a looser definition, the app keeps the 全書's and notes the difference.
  • Tendencies, not verdicts. Classical texts can be blunt. The app words its readings as tendencies, and its own text never predicts death, serious illness or disaster. The classical lines it quotes can be blunter; read them as the tradition's wording, not a forecast.

Calculating the chart

Star placement uses iztro 2.6.3 (MIT licence), pinned to that exact version so charts don't change underneath you. Before iztro runs, the app works out which 時辰 (two-hour period) you were born in:

  1. Clock time to true solar time. The birthplace's historical UTC offset (including daylight saving at the time) gives universal time. Adding 4 minutes per degree of longitude east gives local mean time, and the equation of time (NOAA formula) adjusts that for the season. You can turn this off in Advanced settings to use clock time.
  2. Solar time to 時辰. 子 is 23:00–01:00, 丑 01:00–03:00, and so on. Births within 10 minutes of a boundary show a warning with both possibilities.
  3. Lunar date. iztro converts the solar date to the lunar calendar. The year turns at Lunar New Year, and the late 子 hour (23:00–24:00) counts as the next day; both can be changed.

If you only know the 時辰, choose it directly; no solar-time correction is applied.

How the analysis works

Stars and palaces

Each palace is read from its main stars (borrowed from the opposite palace when it has none), their brightness, the minor stars with them, and any transformation. Readings come from the 全書's descriptions of each star and the standard double-star combinations, plus the 全書 卷二 lines for each star in each palace.

Patterns (格局)

The app checks every named pattern against the chart. An auspicious pattern is graded by what meets it in the Life palace's 三方四正:

  • Formed: the structure, supporting stars (六吉, 祿存 or 祿/權/科), and no malefics.
  • Flawed (破格): supporting stars, but 四煞, 空劫 or 化忌 as well. The promise shows but is only partly delivered.
  • Weak: the structure without supporting stars.
  • Broken: the structure meeting malefics with no support.

A challenging pattern is either Present or Softened, when lucky stars mitigate it as the classics allow.

Palace ratings

Each palace is rated Well supported, Mixed, Under pressure or Quiet. The palace's own stars count most: its lucky stars and 祿/權/科 add, its malefics subtract, and a birth 化忌 in it subtracts heavily. Stars in its 三方四正 count a quarter as much, and 地空 or 地劫 bearing on it (same palace, flanking, trine or opposite) weakens it. The weights were chosen against palace ratings published by two teachers (see checks).

10-year periods and years

Each 大限 is read from the natal palace it falls on, that palace's stars, and the period's own 四化. Where the period's 化忌 lands on the birth 化忌 it doubles the obstruction (疊忌); where its 化祿 meets the birth 化祿 or 祿存 it doubles the gain (疊祿). Patterns are re-checked with the period palace as the Life palace. Years are read the same way, with the year's 化忌 stacking on both the birth and period ones. Each year has two palaces: the 流年 palace (the year's branch) and the 小限, an age palace that moves one palace a year from a start set by your birth year (全書 卷二 安小限訣). The 小限 reaching your birth 化祿, 化權 or 化忌 counts in the year's rating, quoting the 全書's own 小限 lines, and the app notes when the 大限, 小限 and 流年 meet in one palace. 倪海廈 and 范振木 both read yearly events through the 小限. Each period and year shows how its rating was added up.

Elements and zodiac

The overview names your zodiac animal (the birth-year branch), the chart's 五行局 and the elements of your Life palace stars, each quoted from the 全書. It also gives the element's traditional colour (五色) as cultural background only: the 紫微 classics prescribe no colours, and the app offers no colour or feng shui remedies. Year readings note your 本命年 and clash years (沖太歲), quoting the 全書 卷三 where it names them for your branch; these notes don't change the year's rating.

Advice notes

Some readings carry an Advice note on what to do about a hard period or star (趨吉避凶). Each one comes from the 全書 or from a published case reading by a named teacher, and is cited:

  • Hard periods: the 全書 on 陰騭: quietly helping others and correcting your own conduct carries you through.
  • 陀羅 in a period: 「限遇陀羅事亦多，必然忍耐要謙和」 (全書 卷二).
  • 化忌 in the Siblings or Friends palace: no joint ventures or lending with them (倪海廈, 范振木).
  • 化忌 on the Spouse palace in a year: marry after it has passed (倪海廈).

Feng shui remedies and anything claiming to avert illness or death are left out.

Sources

Classical texts (quoted)

  • 《紫微斗數全書》, attributed to 陳希夷 and compiled in the Ming dynasty, volumes 1–3. Public domain; quoted from the Wikisource transcription.
  • 《紫微斗數》, attributed to 陳摶, in the Ming 萬曆 《續道藏》. Public domain; used for a few star descriptions.

Every classical quotation in the app is checked by a script against these transcriptions, including the section it is attributed to, and the verified list is kept with the tests. The test suite, which runs before every deploy, fails if any quoted line is not on that list.

Modern teachers and case studies (cited, not quoted)

  • 范振木 (占驗派), worked cases at 逸雲閣星相研究所: period readings, 夾 and 空劫 techniques, 天馬 by month.
  • 倪海廈, 天紀 lectures 10–15, from students' notes at finalhopes.com: case readings, career and timing advice, his 庚 and 壬 transformations.
  • kunde.org.tw 紫微案例故事, 「淑真的廉貞故事」: a fully printed chart with brightness and palace-by-palace ratings.
  • mingli.study: Life-palace stars and pattern labels for public figures.
  • A modern 格局 catalogue (59 patterns with formation and break conditions), used for structural conditions only.

These works are copyrighted, so the app paraphrases them in its own words and cites them by name and article.

How it was checked

Published cases rarely give exact birth times, so most checks work backwards: find every chart that matches what the case states, then test each claim against those charts.

  • Chart placement. The kunde.org.tw printout matches star for star, including every brightness, the 五行局, 命主, 身主 and the period order. 范振木's three cases with birth data match in every palace once his dates are read as lunar dates.
  • 倪海廈's cases. Of 18 cases in lectures 10 and 12–15, 15 reproduce every stated chart feature; the other three each have one claim that never occurs (likely slips in the notes or a different brightness table).
  • Patterns. Against the pattern labels mingli.study gives for 39 reproducible public figures, agreement rose from 21 to 49 labels after aligning definitions with the 全書. Remaining differences are mostly the site using looser definitions than the 全書.
  • Palace ratings. Against 20 palace ratings published by kunde.org.tw and 范振木, the app's rating matches 10 exactly and never gives the opposite rating.
  • A problem in the 全書 itself. Its definitions of 財蔭夾印 and 財印夾祿 can't occur as written (0 in 4,800 generated charts), because 天相's neighbours are fixed. Swapping the flanking stars between the two definitions makes both workable; the app uses that reading and says so.

These checks are kept as automated tests, so a later change that breaks one of them fails the build. The full write-up is in the project's cross-check notes.

Limits

  • Birth time matters. A different 時辰 is a different chart. If you're unsure, compare the charts for the neighbouring periods.
  • One method. The app reads the 三合 way. It does not yet use 飛星 self-transformations (自化), 斗君, monthly periods (流月) or compatibility between two charts.
  • Brightness tables differ. Schools disagree on some brightness values (for example 火星); the app uses iztro's.
  • No verified life events. The case studies confirm that rules are applied as teachers apply them, not that the readings come true.
  • Privacy. Everything is calculated in your browser. Only the place name you type is sent to the place search; your birth data stays on your device.
